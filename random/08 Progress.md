Have any other startup founders ran into the issue of progress being invisible to others but completely apparent to themselves?

My advice would have to be: Don't ever start a business for yourself, work for someone else (first, to pay bills).

De-risk yourself by joining someone with something already in motion.

I have spent so long at $0 in revenue

Progress has been, from 2023-2026, in the form of being able to build things easier and quicker, having a better understanding of the market I'm working in, and having a better product solution (with no users).

I'm knowingly being extremely negative and I know this is some of the muddying all founders must experience.

But why do I feel so horrible about what I've been able to accomplish?

People have released open-sourced version of the solution I'm building within the last month! But they lack some of the depth in terms of addressing some of the problems I know users face when trying to remix music.

Market understanding is incredibly intangible, it makes me a stronger, more competitive founder in terms of building a music product but means nothing outside of the context of this problem space.

I think I've the acumen now to lead an engineering team, and ingrain the proper user persona to the people building products for our customers. But I have no customers, nor an engineering team!

It's all too superficial, imaginary, and all unpaid! It looks very bad from the outside.

Hopefully someone will read this and relate in the future! Persevere! It's real if you're seeing sober progress. Do not conflate your ideas, are you increasingly poised to win?
